---
title: 'Leg flare balance'
---

Controls the distribution of the flare between the side and inseams.

- Decrease to move the flare to the inseams.
- 100% spreads the flare evenly between the side and inseams.
- Increase to move the flare to the side seams.

:::note

By default the flare is spread more onto the side seams.
It is not recommended to go below 100% as more flare on the inseam can be uncomfortable to wear.

:::
