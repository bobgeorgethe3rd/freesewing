---
title: 'Leg flare lock'
---

Whether the sideseam flare is locked below and is anchored to the fork or it can travel past the fork and is anchored at the waist.

- True the sideseam flare cannot past the fork and is locked mirroring the flare of the inseam.
- False the sideseam flare can travel past the fork and up to the waist.
