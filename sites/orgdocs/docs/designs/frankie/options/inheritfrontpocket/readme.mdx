---
title: 'Inherit front pocket'
---

Due to Frankie's side seams being very versatile you may find when the front pocket drafts in a way that you do not wish to use.
This option switches the draft on whether the front pocket inherits the waist and side seams from the front leg.

- True the front pocket inherits the waist and side seams from the front leg
- False the front pocket does not inherits the waist and side seams from the front leg

:::note

It is only recommended to turn this off when you don't like the shape of the front pocket that is drafted.
When not inheritting you may find that the front pocket pulls the leg in a weord way when anchored in the waist.
To get around this you can do one of the following:

- Convert the pocket so it does not anchor in the waist.
- Trim the top down and use a waist tape to anchor the pocket into the waist. Make sure to give the tape enough slack so it does not pull the leg.

:::
