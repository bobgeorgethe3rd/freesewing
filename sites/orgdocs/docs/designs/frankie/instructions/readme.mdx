:::note

Frankie is based on [FreeSewing's Titan Block](docs/designs/titan).
We recommend making and fitting Titan first before trying Frankie.

:::

### (Optional) Step 1: Prep front pockets

- Press under or _finsih_ the edge of the front pocket facings that does not line up with a seam.
- Pin the front pocket facings to the front pocket bags.
- _Edgestitch_ the pressed or finished edge to the front pocket bag.
- _Baste_ the remaining edges of front pocket facings to the front pocket bags.

### (Optional) Step 2: Attach front pockets to backs

- Pin front pocket bags to backs _good sides together_ along the sideseam matching notches.
- With a seam allowance that is 1/8" (3mm) less than the seam allowance of the side seam, sew the front pocket bags to the backs along the sideseam.
- _Finish_ the edges of the seams.
- Press the front pocket bags away from the centre backs.
- (Recommended) _Edgestitch_ the pressed edges of the font pocket bags.

### Step 3: Sew cross seam

- Sew backs together at cross seam.
- _Finsih_ cross seam.

:::tip

It is recommended to _flat - fell_ or _french seam_ the cross seam for extra strength.

:::

### (Optional) Step 4: Attach front pocket bags to fronts

- Pin front pocket bags to fronts _good sides together_ along the sideseam matching notches.
- With a seam allowance that is 1/8" (3mm) less than the seam allowance of the side seam, sew the front pocket bags to the fronts along the sideseam.
- _Finsih_ the edges of the seams.
- Press the front pocket bags away from the centre fronts.
- (Recommended) _Edgestitch_ the pressed edges of the font pocket bags.

### Step 5: Fly

- If need interface half the fly shield.
- Fold fly shield in half _good sides together_
- Sew bottom edge of fly shield. Trim seam allowance if necessary.
- Turn fly shield _good sides out_. Press.
- If needed _baste_ fly shield's crotch seam.

**If Zipper**

:::fixme

Add Zipper fly step

:::

**If Buttonhole**

- If needed interface half the buttonhole placket.
- Fold the buttonhole placket in half _wrong sides together_.
- If needed _baste_ the raw edges together.
- Sew the buttonholes of the buttonhole placket.
- Trim the right extension off the left leg.
- With _good sides together_ sew the crotch seam of the front legs together from the fork up to the notch. Make sure to _backstitch_ at the notch.
- If needed interface the fly.
- With _good sides together_ sew the fly to the left leg. _Finish_ seam.
- Turn the fly to the _wrong side_.
- _Edgestitch_ or _Understitch_ the fly.
- With _good sides together_ sew the buttonhole placket to the fly.
- _Finish_ the edge of the fly, buttonhole placket and crotch seam.
- From the _good side_, _topstitch_ the fly and buttonhole placket in place using the stitching line as a guide.
- With _good sides together_ sew the fly shield to the right leg.
- Press fly shield to the _wrong side_. _Finish_ the seam.
- Sew the buttons to the fly shield.
- From the _good side_, _bartack_ the bottom of the _topstitching_ making sure to catch the fly shield underneath.
- (Optional) _Topstitch_ from the _good side_ a horizontal line parallel to the waist where the curve of the _topstitching_ starts making sure to catch the fly shield underneath.

### Step 6: Join front to back

:::tip

Now is the time to try it out and see if it fits. Baste the side seams & inseam instead of sewing them and check if you need to make any alterations.

:::

- Pin front and back together at the sideseams.
- Pin the pocket bags together along the curved edge.
- Sew front to back at side seams up to bottom notch of the pocket bag.
- Sew back down to the bottom of the pocket bag stopping the seam allowance away from the bottom. Leave the needle in.
- Pivot the needle then sew off the leg and sew the pocket bags together along the curved edge.
- Sew down the side seam from the top of the waist to the top notch of the pocket bag making sure to _backstitch_ at the end to secure the threads.
- _Finish_ the side seams and pocket bag where sewn.
- _Baste_ the top of the pocket bag to the front waist.
- If not using front pockets simply sew the sideseams and _finish_ them.
- Sew front to back at inseam.
- _Finsih_ the inseam.

:::tip

It is recommended to _flat - fell_ or _french seam_ the inseam for extra strength.

:::

### (Optional) Step 7: Side pockets

#### Top fold type

- (Optional) Interface the fold of the side pockets.
- Press the top seam allowance to the _good side_ of the side pockets.
- Press the top fold to the _wrong side_ of the side pockets.
- Sew the fold to the pocket and the sides.
- Trim the seams you just sewn.
- Clip the top corners.
- Turn the fold _good sides out_. Press.
- Press under the remaining seam allowances of the side pockets.
- (Recommended) _Edgestitch_ the fold in place.

#### Folded in half type

- Fold the side pockets in half _good sides together_.
- Sew around the raw edges of the side pockets making sure to leave a gap for turning.
- Clip the corners & trim the seam allowances.
- Turn _good sides out_.
- Press

#### For both

- Pin the side pockets to the legs matching the bottom edge of the side pockets to the markings.
- _Edgestitch_ the sides of the pockets.
- (Optional) _Topstitch_ the sides of pockets.

:::tip

You can treat the placement markings more as a guideline and can really place these pockets anywhere on the leg you want.

:::

### (Optional) Step 8: Belt loops

- If single folded _finish_ the seam allowance edges of the belt loops.
- Press under the seam allowances.
- If double folded press in half encasing the raw edges on the insides.
- _Edgestitch_ the folded edges.
- Cut along cut lines to create individual belt loops.
- Attach the belt loops to the waist of the body.

:::note

By default Frankie has 5 belt loops, one at each side seam, one at the centre back and one at the centre of each front leg.
Placement varies dependebt on the amount of belt loops you have made.
The lowest available from Frankie is 3, one at each side seam, one at the centre back.
If you always start with these 3 it is easier to figure out the rest.

:::

### Step 9: Waistband

:::note

The instructions are the same for both the curved and straight waistbands. They only differ if you are using a folded straight waistband.

:::

- Interface one waistband piece. (If folded interface half the waistband along the lengthwise)
- Press under the seam allowance of one of the lengthwise edges of the waistband piece that is not interfaced. (If folded the side that is not interfaced)
- With _good sides together_ sew the interfaced waistband to the legs (interface edge if folded), matching the notches to the centre back, sideseams and centre front.Trim seam. (If using make sure to catch the belt loops whilst sewing this seam.)
- Press the waistband and seam allowances up and away from the legs.
- If folded, fold waistband _good sides together_.
- With _good sides together_ sew the non-interfaced waistband piece to along the short edges and the raw long edge (only short edges for folded waistband). Trim seam allowance and clip corners.
- Turn the waistband _good sides out_. Press. Pin in place if needed.
- From the _good side_, _edgestitch_ the waistband along all edges and seam securing the waistband to the legs.
- Alternatively _whipstitch_ or _slipstitch_ the waistband to the wrong side of the legs.
- Sew the button and buttonhole on the waistband.
- Alternatively sew the hook and bar/eye to the waistband
- If using press under a 1 / 4" (6mm) of the belt loops and press up and pin to the waistband.
- _Edgestitch_ the belt loops to the waistband.
- Press the belt loops and _edgestitch_ the fold to the legs. (Keep and eye on where the front pockets are when sewing belt loops to the front and side seams)

:::note

You may find your fabric doesn't require interfacing and can skip that step.
If using buttons it is still recommended to interface the part of the fabric that has the buttonhole.

:::

### Step 10: Hem

:::tip

Due to the curved nature of Frankie's hem you may wish to hang Frankie up for a few days and address any mishapping that occurs.

:::

- Hem the trouser legs using your preferred hemming method.

:::note

If you set the [Hem width](../options/hemwidth) to 0% you can bind the edge rather than hem.

If you are having/have trouble hemming curved seams you may wish to instad finsih the hem with a facing.

- Trace off the bottom edges of the trouser legs by your chosen facing width (we recommend anywhere from 5cm (2") to 25cm (10") + seam allowance, make sure to trace / add the seam and hem allowances)
- Cut the facing pieces either out of the remenants of your main fabric or whatever you have in your stash (Do not be afraid to _piece_ the facings, this will be on the inside after all)
- (Optional) Interface the facing pieces. (This can help keep the flare out but it's really down to you preference and fabric)
- With _good sides together_ sew the front facings to the back facings.
- Press under the seam allowance of the top facings.
- With _good sides together_ sew the facings to the trouser legs matching the hems.
- Trim the hem allowance.
- Turn the facings to the _wrong side_. Press.
- Sew the top edge of the facings down to the trouser legs. (We recommend doing this by hand but it can be done by machine if you don't mind visible topstitching)

:::

### Step 11: Enjoy!

That's it you're all done! Now go and show off your beautifully flared trousers or if your Frankie is very flared chuckle at the knowledge you are wearing secret pants!
