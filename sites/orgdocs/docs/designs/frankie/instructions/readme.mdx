:::note

Frankie is based on [FreeSewing's Titan Block](docs/designs/titan).
We recommend making and fitting Titan first before trying Frankie.

:::

### (Optional) Step 1: Prep front pockets

- Press under or _finsih_ the edge of the front pocket facings that does not line up with a seam.
- Pin the front pocket facings to the front pocket bags.
- _Edgestitch_ the pressed or finished edge to the front pocket bag.
- _Baste_ the remaining edges of front pocket facings to the front pocket bags.

### (Optional) Step 2: Attach front pockets to backs

- Pin front pocket bags to backs _good sides together_ along the sideseam matching notches.
- With a seam allowance that is 1/8" (3mm) less than the seam allowance of the side seam, sew the front pocket bags to the backs along the sideseam.
- _Finish_ the edges of the seams.
- Press the front pocket bags away from the centre backs.
- (Recommended) _Edgestitch_ the pressed edges of the font pocket bags.

### Step 3: Sew cross seam

- Sew backs together at cross seam.
- _Finsih_ cross seam.

:::tip

It is recommended to _flat - fell_ or _french seam_ the cross seam for extra strength.

:::

### (Optional) Step 4: Attach front pocket bags to fronts

- Pin front pocket bags to fronts _good sides together_ along the sideseam matching notches.
- With a seam allowance that is 1/8" (3mm) less than the seam allowance of the side seam, sew the front pocket bags to the fronts along the sideseam.
- _Finsih_ the edges of the seams.
- Press the front pocket bags away from the centre fronts.
- (Recommended) _Edgestitch_ the pressed edges of the font pocket bags.

### Step 6: Join front to back

:::tip

Now is the time to try it out and see if it fits. Baste the side seams & inseam instead of sewing them and check if you need to make any alterations.

::tip

- Pin front and back together at the sideseams.
- Pin the pocket bags together along the curved edge.
- Sew front to back at side seams up to bottom notch of the pocket bag.
- Sew back down to the bottom of the pocket bag stopping the seam allowance away from the bottom. Leave the needle in.
- Pivot the needle then sew off the leg and sew the pocket bags together along the curved edge.
- Sew down the side seam from the top of the waist to the top notch of the pocket bag making sure to _backstitch_ at the end to secure the threads.
- _Finish_ the side seams and pocket bag where sewn.
- _Baste_ the top of the pocket bag to the front waist.
- If not using front pockets simply sew the sideseams and _finish_ them.
- Sew front to back at inseam.
- _Finsih_ the inseam.

:::tip

It is recommended to _flat - fell_ or _french seam_ the inseam for extra strength.

::tip

### (Optional) Step 8: Belt loops

- If single folded _finish_ the seam allowance edges of the belt loops.
- Press under the seam allowances.
- If double folded press in half encasing the raw edges on the insides.
- _Edgestitch_ the folded edges.
- Cut along cut lines to create individual belt loops.
- Attach the belt loops to the waist of the body.

:::note

By default Frankie has 5 belt loops, one at each side seam, one at the centre back and one at the centre of each front leg.
Placement varies dependebt on the amount of belt loops you have made.
The lowest available from Frankie is 3, one at each side seam, one at the centre back.
If you always start with these 3 it is easier to figure out the rest.

:::

### Step 10: Hem

:::tip

Due to the curved nature of Frankie's hem you may wish to hang Frankie up for a few days and address any mishapping that occurs.

:::

- Hem the trouser legs using your preferred hemming method.

:::note

If you set the [Hem width](../options/hemwidth) to 0% you can bind the edge rather than hem.

If you are having/have trouble hemming curved seams you may wish to instad finsih the hem with a facing. - Trace off the bottom edges of the trouser legs by your chosen facing width (we recommend anywhere from 5cm (2") to 25cm (10") + seam allowance, make sure to trace / add the seam and hem allowances) - Cut the facing pieces either out of the remenants of your main fabric or whatever you have in your stash (Do not be afraid to _piece_ the facings, this will be on the inside after all) - (Optional) Interface the facing pieces. (This can help keep the flare out but it's really down to you preference and fabric) - With _good sides together_ sew the front facings to the back facings. - Press under the seam allowance of the top facings. - With _good sides together_ sew the facings to the trouser legs matching the hems. - Trim the hem allowance. - Turn the facings to the _wrong side_. Press. - Sew the top edge of the facings down to the trouser legs. (We recommend doing this by hand but it can be done by machine if you don't mind visible topstitching)
:::
